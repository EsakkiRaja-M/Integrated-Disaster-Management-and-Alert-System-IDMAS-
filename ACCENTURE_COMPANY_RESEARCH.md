2. "What technologies/tools will I be working with initially?"
3. "How are project assignments decided for new joiners?"
4. "What's the team structure I'll be part of?"

### About Growth
5. "What does the career progression path look like for this role?"
6. "How does Accenture support continuous learning and certifications?"
7. "Are there opportunities to rotate between different industries or technology areas?"
8. "What mentorship programs are available for new employees?"

### About Culture
9. "How does Accenture maintain work-life balance, especially during project deadlines?"
10. "What does hybrid work look like for this role?"
11. "How does the company foster innovation among junior employees?"
12. "What makes Accenture different from other consulting firms in terms of employee experience?"
