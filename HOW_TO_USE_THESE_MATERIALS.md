## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't memorize word-for-word** - Understand concepts, speak naturally
2. **Don't say "I don't know" without following up** - Add "but I can learn" or "here's my thinking"
3. **Don't undersell your project** - It's impressive, present it confidently
4. **Don't skip company research** - Shows lack of genuine interest
5. **Don't forget to ask questions** - Shows engagement and interest
6. **Don't forget follow-up email** - Professional courtesy matters
