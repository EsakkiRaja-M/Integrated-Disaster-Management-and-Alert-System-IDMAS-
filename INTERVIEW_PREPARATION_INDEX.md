## 🔥 Top 10 Must-Know Points

Before your interview, ensure you can confidently explain these:

1. **Project Summary (30 seconds)**
   - What: Disaster management system
   - Key features: Automated scheduler, email alerts
   - Technologies: Java, MySQL, JavaMail API
   - Impact: 70% reduction in manual work

2. **StatusScheduler How It Works**
   - Timer/TimerTask classes
   - Delay calculation
   - Database update on schedule
   - Thread safety considerations

3. **Email System Implementation**
   - JavaMail API with Gmail SMTP
   - Bulk sending to 100+ users
   - 95%+ delivery rate
   - Authentication challenges solved

4. **Database Schema**
   - 6 tables in 3NF
   - dis_info, user_login, admin_login, resources, events, event_registrations
   - Foreign key relationships
   - PreparedStatement for security

5. **Biggest Technical Challenge**
   - Learning JavaMail API independently
   - Handling SMTP authentication
   - Ensuring emails don't go to spam
   - Solution: Research, iterative testing

6. **Security Awareness**
   - ✅ Using PreparedStatement (SQL injection prevention)
   - ❌ Plain text passwords (know it's wrong, can explain fix: BCrypt)
   - ❌ Hardcoded credentials (know better: environment variables)
   - Shows mature understanding

7. **What You'd Improve**
   - Password hashing (BCrypt)
   - Connection pooling (HikariCP)
   - Unit tests (JUnit)
   - Separate business logic (DAO pattern)
   - Move to web (Spring Boot + React)

8. **Key Metrics**
   - 70% reduction in manual coordination
   - 2-3 hours → 10 minutes notification time
   - 100+ users supported
   - 95%+ email delivery rate
   - 6 database tables with referential integrity

9. **Why Accenture**
   - $3B AI investment (technology leadership)
   - Global exposure (120+ countries)
   - Learning culture (Accenture Academy)
   - Project diversity (multiple industries)
   - Values alignment (ethics, diversity)

10. **Your Unique Value**
    - Quick learner (learned JavaMail independently)
    - Full-stack capability (GUI, backend, database, integration)
    - Business understanding (not just code, but problem-solving)
    - Security awareness (knows best practices)
    - Growth mindset (can articulate improvements)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### 🎯 Focus Areas by Role

**For Associate Software Engineer:**
- Emphasize learning agility
- Show foundational skills (Java, SQL)
- Demonstrate problem-solving approach
- Express enthusiasm for learning

**For Advanced Associate Software Engineer:**
- Emphasize technical depth (StatusScheduler details)
- Show independent problem-solving
- Discuss design decisions and tradeoffs
- Demonstrate maturity (knowing improvements)

**For Advanced Application Engineer:**
- Emphasize end-to-end ownership
- Show business understanding
- Discuss complete application lifecycle
- Demonstrate practical delivery focus

### 🚀 Success Strategies

1. **Be Authentic:** Don't memorize scripts, understand concepts
2. **Show Enthusiasm:** Genuine excitement about technology matters
3. **Be Honest:** "I don't know but can learn" is better than faking
4. **Ask Questions:** Shows genuine interest and engagement
5. **Connect Dots:** Link your experience to role requirements
6. **Stay Positive:** Focus on what you learned, not just what went wrong
7. **Take Notes:** Shows professionalism and interest

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Saying your project is "simple" or "basic"
2. ❌ Not knowing your own code
3. ❌ Not researching the company
4. ❌ Giving one-word answers
5. ❌ Not asking any questions
6. ❌ Being too nervous to show personality
7. ❌ Forgetting to follow up after interview
